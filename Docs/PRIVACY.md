# Privacy Policy — Interstellar Atlas

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Interstellar Atlas is an interactive astronomy and space exploration application developed for Meta Quest devices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application processes and how that information is handled.

## 2. Information Collection

Interstellar Atlas does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

The application does not collect or transmit personal information to its developers.

All astronomical datasets and application resources are packaged with the application and processed locally on the user's device.

The application may store local settings and preferences on the device. These are not transmitted to the developers.

## 3. Analytics and Diagnostics

Interstellar Atlas does not use advertising services, third-party analytics, or a developer-operated crash-reporting service.

Unity Diagnostics is not currently collecting diagnostic data through Unity Cloud because the project is not connected to Unity Cloud.

No diagnostic reports are intentionally transmitted by the application to its developers.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Interstellar Atlas does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Meta may independently collect and process information through its Meta Quest hardware, operating system, platform services, and associated features.

Such processing is governed by Meta's own privacy policy:

https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/

## 5. Data Storage and Deletion

Interstellar Atlas does not operate remote servers or databases containing user information.

Any application preferences stored locally remain on the user's device and can be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application.

The developers do not maintain personal user information on external servers.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change.

Any updates will be published at this URL, together with a revised update date.
